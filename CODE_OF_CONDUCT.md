# Community conduct

Treat contributors respectfully. Welcome beginners and language learners. Critique work with specific, constructive feedback.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, spam and publishing someone else's private information are not acceptable. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation to protect the community.

For a moderation concern, use GitHub's reporting tools or contact a repository maintainer through their public GitHub contact channel. Do not repeat private material in a public report.
